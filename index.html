About

This is contra-ethos, an independent studio run by me, Ethan Porter; it's my blog and a hub for the projects I build in public.

I love building agentic tooling for developers, tiny games, interactive fiction, and experiments in user experience, game design, and AI. Writing is where my excitement about AI, design, philosophy, and whatever else ends up.

The name is mostly an aesthetic choice. Read "contra" as 'against' and "ethos" as 'a sense of authority', which fits my love for decentralization and my non-conformist values.
